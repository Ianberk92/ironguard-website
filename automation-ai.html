Why it matters

Good IT removes decisions from employees instead of adding more.

Every process that depends on someone remembering the steps will eventually be done differently, late, or not at all. And every month, new AI tools enter the company through individual sign-ups: useful, fast-moving, and invisible to leadership until something goes wrong.

Our position

AI is an operations question before it is a technology question.

The companies that get value from AI are the ones that can answer three questions:

  • Which tools are in use, and by whom.
  • What data they touch.
  • What job they are doing.

That is governance, and it is the same ownership discipline we bring to everything else.
