New-Site IT Launch Checklist

Open the new office or job site with IT ready on day one.

The internet circuit is usually the longest lead-time item in a move, and the one most often ordered last. Put these five dates on the same schedule as construction and furniture, each with an owner.

90+

At lease signing (90 days out or more)

  • Confirm which carriers serve this building and this suite
  • Order the primary internet circuit; get the install date and its dependencies in writing
  • Choose the site type from your standard (small office, full office, job-site trailer)
  • Name one owner for the IT launch
Date: circuit ordered ________ Owner ________
60

60 days out

  • Order firewall, switches, wireless access points and licenses
  • Order a cellular backup connection (failover, and a bridge if the circuit slips)
  • Put low-voltage cabling on the contractor’s schedule, before walls and ceilings close
  • Confirm dedicated power and airflow for the network closet
Dates: cabling rough-in ________ hardware on hand ________
30

30 days out

  • Configure and test all network gear off-site, to your standard, and label it
  • Get the people list: who moves, who is new, what each needs on day one
  • Plan printers and plotters, conference rooms, door access, cameras
  • Start the site record: circuit IDs, carrier support numbers, device names
Date: staging complete ________
7

7 days out

  • Install, then unplug the primary circuit and confirm the site stays up on backup
  • Walk the space with a laptop and check wireless where people will sit
  • Photograph and label the network closet
  • Tell employees how to get help; same support path as every other site
Date: cutover and failover test ________
1

Opening day and week one

  • A named person on site or on call, not a ticket queue
  • Keep a punch list and close every item within the week
  • File the final site record where the next person can find it
Punch list closed ________

For a job-site trailer or temporary site

Same checklist, compressed: cellular or satellite as the primary connection, a small pre-configured kit, the same security settings and the same support path as the main office. Skip nothing in the 7-day section.
