When this fits

The machines keep running. The paperwork keeps coming.

Manufacturers deal with two kinds of pressure at once. On the floor, machines and controllers run old software on a flat network, and when the ERP stops, so does shipping. In the office, a prime contractor asks for your SPRS score, a CMMC deadline is written into the next contract, and a customer sends a security audit with a hundred questions.

None of that fits on the plate of the person who also orders the toner.

We take it over and run it as one system. We get you ready for assessment, keep the floor and the office apart, and keep the systems that ship product running. Ironguard IT is based in Carlsbad, in Southern California, and supports companies across California.

What we take on

Ready for the assessment. Steady on the floor.

Managed service starts at 25 users. If you have an internal IT lead or a controls engineer, we work through them, as owned lanes, not around them.

CMMC and compliance

  • Your CMMC 2.0 and NIST SP 800-171 program run end to end, from gap assessment to assessment day
  • System Security Plan (SSP), POA&M, policies and procedures written and kept current
  • Microsoft 365 GCC High or a scoped enclave for controlled unclassified information
  • MFA everywhere, central logging, continuous monitoring and the evidence an assessor asks for
  • Customer audits, supply-chain questionnaires and TISAX preparation for automotive work

Floor, network and people

  • Shop-floor (OT) networks separated from the office, with only the connections production needs
  • ERP and MES uptime: servers, backups, licensing and the vendor relationship
  • Backup and disaster recovery, with restores tested and written down
  • Onboarding and offboarding for office staff and shift workers
  • Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace administration and licensing
Your first 90 days with us

What happens after you hire us.

A typical start. Your own plan is written down, with names and dates, before any work begins.

  1. Week 1

    We walk the floor before we write a plan.

    • Tour the plant, the office and any second site with your team
    • Map where controlled unclassified information (CUI) actually lives
    • List every machine PC, controller and server, and who owns it
    • Take over admin accounts and put them in your company’s name

    Where you are: A written map of your environment and of where CUI flows.

  2. Days 1–30

    Close the gaps that matter most.

    • MFA on every account, starting with email, remote access and admins
    • ERP and file servers backed up, and a real restore tested
    • A CMMC and NIST SP 800-171 gap assessment, requirement by requirement
    • A decision on scope: GCC High, an enclave, or neither

    Where you are: You know your real readiness, in writing, and what it will take.

  3. Days 31–60

    Build the controls and the paperwork.

    • Shop-floor machines moved onto their own network
    • Central logging and continuous monitoring switched on
    • Your System Security Plan, POA&M, policies and procedures written
    • Devices enrolled, encrypted and patched on a schedule

    Where you are: The floor is protected, and your evidence is in one place.

  4. Days 61–90

    Get ready to be asked.

    • A mock assessment with your team, and remediation of anything it finds
    • Your SPRS score updated from real evidence
    • A security pack for customer audits and supply-chain questionnaires
    • A 12-month roadmap and your first quarterly review

    Where you are: When a prime or a customer asks, you have answers and proof.

Common questions

Straight answers for manufacturers and defense suppliers.

Can you get us CMMC certified?

We run your CMMC and NIST SP 800-171 program end to end: gap assessment, the System Security Plan and POA&M, putting the controls in place and managing them, evidence collection, continuous monitoring, remediation and support on assessment day. We use our own team plus specialist partners where needed, and we coordinate the accredited assessor (C3PAO). By law the certification itself is issued by that assessor, not by us.

Do we need Microsoft 365 GCC High?

It depends on your contracts and the data you handle. Some companies need GCC High for controlled unclassified information; others can use a smaller enclave. We look at your contracts and data flows, recommend one in writing, then plan and run the move.

Will you touch our machines and controllers?

Only with your controls team. We separate the shop floor from the office network and limit what each side can reach. Changes to machines and controllers are planned with your team and the equipment vendor, and scheduled around production.

Can you help with customer audits and TISAX?

Yes. We answer customer audits and supply-chain questionnaires with you, backed by real evidence. For automotive work we prepare you for TISAX end to end, run the controls and evidence, and coordinate the assessment with an approved audit provider, who issues the result. Policy and audit support is included in the Managed + Security level; on other levels it is scoped in writing.

What does it cost?

Three published levels, $115 to $200 per user per month. Companies working toward CMMC usually fit the top level, Managed + Security. Hardware, GCC High migrations and other major projects are scoped separately, in writing, before work begins.
