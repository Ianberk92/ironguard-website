The promise

You will know who owns the problem, what is happening, and what comes next.

That sentence is the whole business model. Every standard we set, every report we send, and every project we run exists to make it true, on a normal Tuesday and in the middle of an incident.

Why companies switch to us

Five differences you can check.

Every IT provider says “proactive.” These are the specifics, in terms you can hold us to.

01

An operator, not a help desk.

We run IT the way an internal IT department does, because that is where we come from.

  • Our founder spent a decade leading IT inside engineering and construction companies.
  • Today we run the whole IT function for a multi-site company with offices, labs, warehouses and field sites in several states.
02

One standard for every site.

Head office, branch, lab, warehouse or job site: the same rules, written down.

  • A written site network standard every location is built to.
  • New sites launch on a dated checklist, not on phone hotspots.
  • Every cutover has a rollback plan before it starts.
03

Ownership in writing.

Responsibility you can point to, not a promise in a sales meeting.

  • A named owner for your account, and 26 written operating procedures.
  • What is not covered is written down too: hardware, travel, dedicated onsite coverage and defined projects, each approved in writing first.
  • You own every credential and account. 90-day start, then month to month on 60 days’ notice.
04

Fast, senior response.

The people who answer know your company and can act.

  • Our service desk: a median first response of about 8 minutes (November 2026).
  • No tier-1 script readers. Every inquiry reaches someone with the authority to act.
05

Published prices.

One more thing you can check before a call, not the whole story.

  • Three published levels, $115 to $200 per user per month, with what each includes.
  • Your rate is fixed in writing after a scoped review. See the levels.
In writing, not in a sales deck

Standards we commit to in every managed agreement.

  • Same-day offboardingEvery account and access removed the day someone leaves.
  • Day-one onboardingLaptop, accounts and correct access ready before a new hire starts.
  • Quarterly restore validationWe restore from backup on a schedule, not just confirm the job ran.
  • Monthly license reconciliationCounts checked against headcount so you never pay for ghosts.
  • Credentials and accounts in your nameAdmin access, documentation and licensing are yours from day one. Nothing to hold hostage.
  • Scoped in writing before work beginsProjects, hardware and onsite coverage are priced up front. No surprise line items.

Honest fit

A strong fit for some companies. Wrong for others.

We would rather say so on the website than find out three months into an agreement.

A strong fit

  • 50–500 employees, with multiple offices, sites, or a distributed workforce.
  • Running on Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, and security-conscious.
  • Growing fast enough that devices, onboarding, licensing, and vendors can no longer be handled informally.
  • Values accountability and clear business communication over the lowest possible price.

Not the right fit

  • Very small companies looking for occasional break-fix help. Managed plans start at 25 users.
  • Buyers choosing purely on the lowest per-user price.
  • Companies that want us to take responsibility but will not agree to standards, access, or decision rights.
  • One-off projects with no strategic fit.
