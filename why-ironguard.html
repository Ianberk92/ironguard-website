Honest fit

A strong fit for some companies. Wrong for others.

We would rather say so on the website than find out three months into an agreement.

A strong fit

  • 50–500 employees, with multiple offices, sites, or a distributed workforce.
  • Running on Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, and security-conscious.
  • Growing fast enough that devices, onboarding, licensing, and vendors can no longer be handled informally.
  • Values accountability and clear business communication over the lowest possible price.

Not the right fit

  • Very small companies looking for occasional break-fix help. Managed plans start at 25 users.
  • Buyers choosing purely on the lowest per-user price.
  • Companies that want us to take responsibility but will not agree to standards, access, or decision rights.
  • One-off projects with no strategic fit.

Promises you can hold in writing.
