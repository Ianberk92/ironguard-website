How we behave

No vague handoffs. No disappearing behind another vendor.

We speak business.

We work where the business works.

We set standards.

We stay involved.

The relationship does not disappear once the agreement is signed.

Honest fit

We are a strong fit for some companies and wrong for others.

A strong fit

75–500 employees. Multiple offices, sites, or a distributed workforce. Microsoft 365-centered and security-conscious. Growing fast enough that devices, onboarding, licensing, and vendors can no longer be handled informally. Values accountability and clear business communication over the lowest possible price.

Not the right fit

Very small companies looking for occasional break-fix help. Buyers choosing purely on the lowest per-user price. Companies that want us to take responsibility but will not agree to standards, access, or decision rights. One-off projects with no strategic fit.

The Ownership Commitments

Promises you can hold in writing, not vibes.

Anyone can claim accountability. These are the commitments that make it checkable.

Our pricing is published.

It starts at $150 per user per month, on the website, with what it includes, what it does not, and what moves the number. Almost nobody in this industry will show you a price. We just did.

Your credentials are yours. Always.

Admin credentials, documentation, and licensing live in your name from day one. If we ever part ways, there is nothing to hold hostage.

No surprise line items.

Exactly four things are billed outside the baseline — hardware, travel, dedicated onsite coverage, and defined projects — and each is approved in writing before it happens.

A named owner for every area.

Support, security, cloud, devices, sites, vendors: each has one accountable name, and leadership always knows whose it is.

The founder reads every inquiry.

And answers it. No SDR sequence, no handoff to a closer.

Response commitments, with teeth.

Urgent issues: response within one business hour. Standard requests: same business day. Miss it, and you hear it from us first — with what happened and what we changed.
