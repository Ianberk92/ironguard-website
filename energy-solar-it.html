Energy & solar

IT for energy and solar companies, from the office to the field site.

For energy and solar technology companies spread across offices, labs, warehouses and field sites: one network standard for every location, lab and operational networks kept apart from corporate, and a named owner for every part of your technology.

When this fits

Many sites, and no two alike.

Energy companies rarely have one kind of location. There is the office, then a lab, a warehouse, a field site in another state, and test equipment that needs its own network. Hiring jumps when a project lands. Partners and utilities send security questionnaires that need real answers, with evidence.

Each of those can be handled one at a time. The trouble starts when nobody owns all of them together.

This is work we do today. Ironguard IT supports multi-site companies with offices, labs, warehouses and field sites: firewalls and networks on one standard at every site, lab and operational networks kept separate from the corporate one, Microsoft 365, and security reviews. Ironguard IT is based in Carlsbad, in Southern California, and supports companies across California.

What we take on

Every site on one standard. Every network in its lane.

Managed service starts at 25 users, anywhere in California. If you have an internal IT lead, we work through them, as owned lanes, not around them.

Sites and networks

  • One firewall and network standard for offices, labs, warehouses and field sites
  • Lab, test and operational (OT) networks kept separate from the corporate network
  • New sites launched from a dated launch checklist
  • Internet circuits, failover and the carrier relationship
  • Every closet documented, labeled and photographed

People and security

  • Onboarding and offboarding that keep pace when project hiring picks up
  • Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace administration and licensing
  • A security baseline: identity and MFA, devices, email and backup
  • Answers and evidence for partner and utility security questionnaires
  • Help preparing for cyber insurance applications and renewals
How we work

Built for sites that do not look alike.

The same four commitments on every engagement.

01

One standard for every site

Same firewall platform, same network design, same documentation rule, whether the site is an office or a warehouse. See how that worked for a multi-site operator.

02

Separate networks for separate jobs

Lab and operational equipment sits on its own network, with only the connections the work needs, so a problem on one side stays on that side.

03

Pricing you can see

Managed IT is $115 to $200 per user per month, across three published levels. Hardware, travel to field sites and major projects are scoped separately, in writing.

04

It all stays yours

Credentials, licenses, documentation and vendor accounts are in your company’s name from day one.

Common questions

Straight answers for energy and solar companies.

Do you support labs, warehouses and field sites, not just offices?

Yes. We support companies with offices, labs, warehouses and field sites today, with the firewalls and networks at every site on one standard.

How do you handle lab and operational (OT) networks?

We keep lab, test and operational networks separate from the corporate network, with only the connections the work needs. A problem on one side stays on that side, and each network can follow the rules its equipment needs.

Can you help answer security questionnaires from partners and utilities?

Yes. Security reviews are part of the work we do for the energy company we support today. Policy and audit support is included in the Managed + Security level; on other levels it is scoped in writing.

Our sites are in more than one state. Is that a problem?

No. Ironguard IT is based in Carlsbad, in Southern California, and supports companies across California. We already support companies with sites in more than one state. Travel to field sites and remote locations is scoped separately, in writing, before it happens.

What does it cost?

Three published levels, $115 to $200 per user per month. Companies with several sites, servers or field crews fit Managed, $150 to $165. Companies with compliance, insurance or partner security requirements fit Managed + Security, $185 to $200.
