The problem

Your IT problem is bigger than the ticket queue.

As companies grow, technology spreads across employees, offices, field sites, vendors, cloud systems, security tools and major projects. The issue is not a lack of activity. It is a lack of ownership.

  • The MSP answers tickets but cannot answer business questions.
  • Onboarding, offboarding, licensing and devices happen differently every time.
  • Security tools exist, but nobody owns the gaps between them.
  • Each new site becomes its own technology island.
  • Leadership spends its time chasing vendors and translating technical answers.
The Ironguard model

From reactive to controlled, in four moves.

We work across the full environment, from everyday employee support to the infrastructure and decisions that keep the business running. We learn how your company works, set clear standards, and own execution across the pieces that normally fall between teams and vendors.

Questions

Straight answers, before you ask.

Are you just a help desk?

No. Employee support is part of the service, but Ironguard also owns security, cloud administration, devices, networks, vendors, lifecycle and major projects.

Do you replace internal IT?

Ironguard can operate as the primary IT function or take ownership of defined areas alongside an internal team. See co-managed IT.

Can you support multiple offices and field sites?

Yes. Multi-site and mixed office, lab, warehouse and field environments are a core part of what we do.

Do you work with Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace?

Both. Microsoft 365 (Entra ID, Intune, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive) and Google Workspace (Cloud Identity, Gmail, Drive, Meet, endpoint management), including security, licensing and governance, are central capabilities.

Is cybersecurity included?

A strong security baseline is built into managed IT. Advanced compliance, testing and project work are scoped based on the environment and requirements.

What happens first?

A working session and an environment review, then a written transition and improvement plan.

Do we have to standardize?

Yes, where it matters. Clear standards are how we can take real responsibility for security, support quality and outcomes.

How much does managed IT cost?

Pricing starts at $150 per user per month, with engagements priced between $150 and $200 per user depending on scope. That covers employee support, a security baseline, Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace administration, device lifecycle, vendor management and reporting. Onsite coverage, hardware, travel and major projects are scoped separately, in writing, before work begins. Managed plans start at 25 users.

The fee also works to earn itself back: a monthly license and spend review is part of the service — seats you no longer use, renewals that should be renegotiated, carriers that should be consolidated.
