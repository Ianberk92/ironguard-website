The problem

Your IT problem is bigger than the ticket queue.

As companies grow, technology spreads across employees, offices, field sites, vendors, cloud systems, security tools, and major projects. The issue is not a lack of activity. It is a lack of ownership.

  • The MSP answers tickets but cannot answer business questions.
  • Onboarding, offboarding, licensing, and devices happen differently every time.
  • Security tools exist, but nobody owns the gaps between them.
  • Each new site becomes its own technology island.
  • Leadership spends too much time chasing vendors and translating technical answers.
The Ironguard model

One operating model. One accountable team.

Ironguard works across the full environment, from everyday employee support to the infrastructure and decisions that keep the business running. We learn how your company works, set clear standards, and own execution across the pieces that normally fall between teams and vendors.

Questions

Straight answers, before you even ask.

Are you just a help desk?

No. Employee support is part of the service, but Ironguard also owns security, cloud administration, devices, networks, vendors, lifecycle, and major projects.

Do you replace internal IT?

Ironguard can operate as the primary IT function or take ownership of defined areas alongside an internal team.

Can you support multiple offices and field sites?

Yes. Multi-site and mixed office, lab, warehouse, and field environments are a core part of the positioning.

Do you work with Microsoft 365?

Yes. Microsoft 365, Entra ID, Intune, Teams, SharePoint, OneDrive, security, licensing, and governance are central capabilities.

Is cybersecurity included?

A strong security baseline is built into managed IT. Advanced compliance, testing, and project work are scoped based on the environment and requirements.

What happens first?

Ironguard starts with a working session and environment review, then provides a clear transition and improvement plan.

Do we have to standardize?

Yes, where it matters. Clear standards are how Ironguard can take real responsibility for security, support quality, and outcomes.
