What IT support costs in California in 2026.

Ironguard IT·Updated November 2026·6 min read

The short answer. The providers we checked across Southern California publish rates from about $60 to $350 per user per month. Ironguard IT has three published levels, $115 to $200 per user per month. The number on its own tells you little: what matters is what each rate includes, and what gets billed on top.

Ask a few IT companies what support costs and you will often hear “it depends,” followed by a discovery call and a proposal weeks later. It does depend, but on things you can name in advance. This guide shows the range providers publish, exactly what each of our levels includes, and the questions that make two quotes comparable.

Ironguard IT is based in Carlsbad, in Southern California, and supports companies across California. The prices and inclusions below are the same wherever you are in the state.

What providers in San Diego and Southern California publish

In November 2026 we read the pricing pages of five IT providers serving San Diego and the rest of Southern California. The per-user rates they publish run from about $60 to $350 per user per month.

That is a range we checked ourselves, not a survey of the whole state. Each provider also covers a different scope for its price, so use the range to orient by, not as a like-for-like comparison.

Publishing a price is no longer rare. Saying exactly what that price buys still is. So here is ours, line by line.

What is scoped separately

Four things sit outside the monthly rate. Each is scoped and approved in advance, so none of them turns up as a surprise on an invoice:

  • Dedicated onsite coverage
  • Hardware and equipment purchases
  • Travel to field sites and remote locations
  • Major projects: buildouts, migrations, relocations

If a provider says all of this is “included,” it has been priced in somewhere. You pay either way; the difference is whether you can see it.

How to compare two quotes

Put each quote next to the list of inclusions above and mark what it covers. A lower rate that leaves out security monitoring, servers or vendor management is not cheaper; it moves those costs into projects and change orders. A very low per-user quote usually signals a coverage decision you have not been told about, such as no security baseline beyond antivirus or “best effort” response. The cheapest per-user price is rarely the cheapest year. It is the same math as the cheapest laptop.

Three questions make any quote comparable:

  1. What exactly is in the monthly rate, in writing?
  2. What are your three most common out-of-scope charges, and what did they cost your clients last year?
  3. What happens to the rate at renewal?

Any provider who answers all three plainly is worth talking to, whatever they charge.

The other costs to plan for

  • Onboarding. A fixed-fee project with a 50% deposit, scoped in writing before work begins, then a fixed monthly rate.
  • Contract terms. Agreements start with a 90-day initial period, then run month-to-month with 60 days’ notice.
  • Insurance renewal on a deadline. The Insurance Readiness Review is a fixed fee: $1,500 for 30–75 users, $2,500 for 76–150 users, larger teams quoted in writing. It is credited in full toward onboarding if you become a managed client within 90 days.
  • The rest of the budget. Laptops, licenses and renewals are separate lines. Your IT budget is missing lines walks through them.
Straight answers

IT support cost questions, answered plainly.

How much does IT support cost per user in San Diego?

The providers we checked across Southern California, San Diego included, publish rates from about $60 to $350 per user per month. Scopes differ a lot, so compare what each rate includes, not the headline number. Ironguard IT has three published levels, $115 to $200 per user per month.

Do you support companies outside San Diego?

Yes. Ironguard IT is based in Carlsbad, in Southern California, and supports companies across California. Our three published levels are the same everywhere; onsite coverage and travel to your sites are scoped separately, in writing, before any work begins.

What does Ironguard IT charge?

Three published levels, $115 to $200 per user per month: Essentials from $115, Managed at $150 to $165, and Managed + Security at $185 to $200. The level depends on servers, sites and security requirements, and your rate is fixed in writing after a scoped review.

What is not included in the monthly price?

Four things, each scoped and approved in advance: dedicated onsite coverage, hardware and equipment purchases, travel to field sites and remote locations, and major projects such as buildouts, migrations and relocations.

Is there a minimum company size?

Managed plans start at 25 users. Essentials is for cloud-only companies on one site, under 40 people. Onboarding is a fixed-fee project with a 50% deposit, scoped in writing before work begins.

Are we locked into a long contract?

No. Agreements start with a 90-day initial period, then run month-to-month with 60 days’ notice. Your documentation and credentials belong to you from day one.

Why do IT support quotes vary so much?

Because the scope varies. One rate may include security monitoring, servers and vendor management; another may cover the help desk only and bill the rest as projects. Ask what is in the monthly rate, in writing, before comparing prices.
