Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 20, 2026

Last updated: October 20, 2026

This policy explains what Ironguard IT (“Ironguard,” “we”) collects through ironguardit.com, how we use it, and the choices you have. Ironguard IT is based in Carlsbad, California.

What we collect

Information you give us. When you use our contact form, we collect your name, work email, company size, what is driving your inquiry, and any details you add. When you book a meeting through Microsoft Bookings, we receive the name, email and other details you enter there. When you email or call us, we receive what you send.

Information collected automatically. We use Google Analytics and Google Ads conversion tracking. They use cookies and similar identifiers to record pages viewed, clicks on buttons such as “Book a Meeting,” whether a form was submitted (not what you typed), approximate location, and device and browser details. Our website host and font provider receive your IP address when pages and fonts load.

The IT Scorecard. Your answers are scored in your browser and are not sent to us. If you choose “Send my scorecard to Ironguard,” your own email program opens with your results so you can decide whether to send them.

Client chat. The client support page includes a chat widget for existing clients. Messages sent through it are processed by the chat provider and delivered to our support team.

How we use it

  • To respond to inquiries, schedule meetings and provide services to clients.
  • To understand how the website is used and whether our advertising works.
  • To keep the website and our systems secure.

We do not collect sensitive personal information through this website, and we do not sell personal information for money.

Advertising and cookies

Google Ads conversion tracking tells us when someone who clicked one of our ads later books a meeting or submits the form. Under California law this may be considered “sharing” personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising. You can opt out in any of these ways:

  • Turn on Global Privacy Control in your browser. When our site detects it, advertising cookies are turned off for your visit.
  • Block or delete cookies in your browser settings.
  • Adjust ad personalization at Google Ad Settings, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us operate, and only as needed: Microsoft (email, Microsoft Bookings and the service that delivers form submissions), Google (analytics, advertising measurement and web fonts), GitHub (website hosting), our client chat and support-desk providers, and our accounting provider for clients. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect our rights and systems.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information as long as needed to handle the conversation and for reasonable business records. Client information is kept for the term of the engagement and as required for legal, tax and accounting purposes. Google Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings.

Your rights

You may ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop sharing it for advertising. California residents have these rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act, including the right not to be discriminated against for using them. To make a request, email support@ironguardit.com. We will verify your request using information you have already given us, and you may use an authorized agent.

Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect the information we hold. No website or system is completely secure.

Children

This website is meant for businesses and is not directed to children under 16. We do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.

Contact

Questions about privacy: support@ironguardit.com.
