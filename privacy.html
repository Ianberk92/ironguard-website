Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

Policy

What we collect

When you contact us through this website, we collect the information you provide: your name, work email, company, and the message you send. We do not use tracking that collects sensitive personal information, and we do not sell personal information.

How we use it

We use your contact information to respond to your inquiry and, if you become a client, to deliver services. We use aggregate, non-identifying analytics to understand how the website is used.

How long we keep it

We keep inquiry information as long as needed to handle the conversation and for reasonable business records, then delete it.

Sharing

We do not share your information with third parties except service providers that help us operate (such as email hosting), and only as needed to do so.

Your choices

You may ask us at any time to correct or delete the information we hold about you by emailing support@ironguardit.com.

Questions

Contact support@ironguardit.com with any privacy question.
