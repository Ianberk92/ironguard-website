Why one system

Nobody gets breached inside a service. They get breached between them.

A ticket queue, a security vendor, a cloud consultant, and a cabling contractor can all be doing their jobs — while onboarding still fails, renewals still surprise finance, and the next site launch still has no owner. The failures live in the handoffs. So that is what we own: not six services, but the connections between them.

Have an internal IT team? Co-managed IT takes defined lanes off their plate without taking their job.
