Founder

A simple standard.

Ian Berkowitz built Ironguard around one rule: if a client trusts you with the technology their business depends on, you act like an owner. Understand the company. Give straight answers. Set real standards. Show up when the work is difficult. Stay accountable through the result, not through the ticket.

That conviction comes from the inside. Ian spent a decade in IT leadership for engineering, construction and energy companies, sitting in the rooms where executives, operations, finance, HR and engineers actually make decisions. Most IT providers never see those rooms. Ironguard was built by someone who worked in them.

  • Senior from day one. The person who scopes your environment is the person who runs it.
  • Platform-fluent. Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace done properly, not bolted on.
  • Straight answers. No drip campaigns, no tiered call queues, no promises we can’t keep in writing.
