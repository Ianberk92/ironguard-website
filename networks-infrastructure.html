The pattern

A new office should not become its own technology island.

Growing companies open sites faster than IT standards keep up. Each location ends up with its own ISP contract, its own firewall brand, its own Wi-Fi quirks, and its own undocumented closet. Then the person who set it up leaves, and every outage becomes archaeology.

What we own

From the wall jack to the WAN.

Site networks

Firewalls, switching, Wi-Fi, and segmentation designed once and repeated at every location—office, lab, or warehouse.

Connectivity

ISP selection, failover, VPN, and site-to-site links—with vendors managed by us, not by your operations team.

Cabling & buildouts

Structured cabling, network rooms, and new-site buildouts coordinated with contractors, landlords, and timelines.

Field environments

Connectivity and equipment for field sites and temporary locations, built for real-world constraints—power, weather, distance.

Monitoring & documentation

Every site monitored, every closet documented, every change recorded—so outages are diagnosed, not excavated.

Infrastructure projects

Migrations, relocations, and critical infrastructure work with executive-level ownership from plan through result.

Site launches

Opening a location becomes a checklist, not a project crisis.

Because every site follows one design, a new location is a known quantity: the same network standard, the same security baseline, the same support model. Your team walks in on day one and works.

Next step

Bring us in before the lease is signed. The earlier infrastructure has an owner, the cheaper and calmer it goes.
