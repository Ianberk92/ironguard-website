When this fits

A filing deadline does not wait for IT.

Law firms carry a duty of confidentiality that most businesses never think about. Every matter needs the right people in and everyone else out. Documents must be kept, found and sometimes held. A client’s outside counsel guidelines arrive with security requirements attached, and the malpractice or cyber insurer wants its own answers. Then the document system slows down at 4:30 on filing day.

Your partners should be practicing law, not resetting the conference room screen.

We take that over and run it as one system. Ironguard IT is based in Carlsbad, in Southern California, and supports firms across California.

What we take on

Every matter protected. Every office on one standard.

Managed service starts at 25 users. If you have an office manager or an IT lead, we work through them, as owned lanes, not around them.

Confidentiality and records

  • Matter-level access and ethical walls set up and checked in your document system
  • Document management and retention that keep files findable and ready for e-discovery
  • Legal holds you can place and prove, without losing anything
  • Secure client communication: encrypted email and client file sharing
  • Answers for outside counsel guidelines and insurer security questionnaires

Offices and people

  • Uptime for the systems you file with, with changes scheduled away from deadlines
  • Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace administration and licensing
  • A security baseline: identity and MFA, devices, email and backup
  • Every office and home office on one network and device standard
  • Practice management, billing and document vendors managed for you
Your first 90 days with us

What happens after you hire us.

A typical start. Your own plan is written down, with names and dates, before any work begins.

  1. Week 1

    We learn the firm before we change anything.

    • Meet your managing partner and office manager, and get your deadline calendar
    • List every system that holds client files, and who can open what
    • Check backups, MFA and email security as they are today
    • Take over admin accounts and put them in your firm’s name

    Where you are: A written map of your environment, and the three biggest risks named.

  2. Days 1–30

    Protect client files first.

    • MFA on every account, including partners and remote access
    • Client files backed up, and a real restore tested
    • Access to sensitive matters reviewed, and ethical walls checked
    • Changes scheduled around your filing calendar, never on deadline day

    Where you are: Client files are recoverable, and only the right people can open them.

  3. Days 31–60

    Make the safe way the normal way.

    • Retention and legal hold settings agreed with you and put in place
    • Encrypted email and secure client file sharing your lawyers will use
    • Devices enrolled and encrypted, in every office and at home
    • A new-hire and leaver checklist, so access always matches the matter list

    Where you are: Lawyers share files securely because it is the easy way.

  4. Days 61–90

    Be ready for the questionnaire.

    • A security pack for outside counsel guidelines and insurer renewals
    • Disaster recovery plan written and walked through with the firm
    • Vendor contracts and renewals on one calendar
    • Your first quarterly review: what changed, what is next, what it costs

    Where you are: When a client or insurer asks how you protect their data, you can show them.

Common questions

Straight answers for law firms.

How do you set up ethical walls?

We set up matter-level access in your document management system and file storage, so people outside a wall cannot open, search or be sent those files. Then we review access on a schedule and keep a record your firm can show a client.

Can you help with document retention and e-discovery?

Yes. We set up retention and legal hold settings that match the policy your firm decides, so documents are kept, findable and ready to produce. Your lawyers decide what to keep and hold; we make the systems do it.

Clients send us security requirements in their outside counsel guidelines. Can you help?

Yes. We build a security pack with your policies and the evidence behind them, then help you answer outside counsel guidelines and insurer questionnaires honestly. Policy and audit support is included in the Managed + Security level; on other levels it is scoped in writing.

We have more than one office. Is that a problem?

No. Every office, and every home office, gets the same network, device and security standard, so a lawyer can work the same way from any of them.

What does it cost?

Three published levels, $115 to $200 per user per month. Firms with client security requirements or insurer reviews usually fit the top level, Managed + Security. Hardware, office moves and major projects are scoped separately, in writing, before work begins.
