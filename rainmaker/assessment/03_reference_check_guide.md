# H3. Track Record Verification — Structured Reference Checks

Rules: obtain the candidate's written consent; never ask a reference for a current or former employer's
customer lists, pricing or other confidential data; ask for ranges and rankings rather than account names;
document answers verbatim; three references minimum (a former manager, a peer or sales engineer, and a client
the candidate won — the candidate selects the client and asks permission first).

## Former manager (15–20 min)

1. In what period did you manage [name], and what was the role and territory?
2. What was the quota, and what percentage did [name] attain each year? (ranges are fine)
3. Of [name]'s closed business, roughly what share was personally originated versus inbound or inherited?
4. Where did [name] rank among peers each year? Any formal awards?
5. What was the typical deal size and sales cycle? Which buyer titles did [name] sell to?
6. How did [name] prospect? Was pipeline consistent or lumpy?
7. How did clients [name] won perform after signature: retention at 12 months, expansions, any churn drivers?
8. Describe a deal [name] lost and how they handled it.
9. Did [name] ever discount outside policy or over-promise? How did they handle objections on price?
10. If you were starting a company, would you hire [name] as your first salesperson? As a future sales leader?
11. Anything about integrity, accuracy of reporting or teamwork I should know?
12. Would you rehire? (Listen for hesitation.)

## Peer / sales engineer

1. What was it like to work deals with [name]? Who ran discovery?
2. How technically fluent was [name] on managed services, security and cloud?
3. How did [name] treat delivery teams after the sale?
4. Did [name] win business others could not? Example (no client names needed).

## Client the candidate won

1. How did you first hear from [name], and what made you take the meeting?
2. What problem did [name] help you see differently?
3. Did the promises made during the sale match the delivery?
4. Would you buy from [name] again at a new company?
