# The Opportunity Narrative — Ironguard IT's founding commercial leader

Use: the truthful core of every conversation and personalized message. Everything here is either verifiable on
the public website or explicitly framed as "if/when" with CEO authorization. Do not add claims.

## Where the company is (facts a candidate can check)

- Carlsbad-based managed IT and cybersecurity firm; senior-led; founder with a decade of in-house IT leadership in engineering and construction companies.
- Published pricing ($150–$200 per user per month, 25-user minimum) and a published exclusions list; 90-day initial term then month-to-month; the client owns credentials and documentation from day one.
- Written operating commitments (same-day offboarding, day-one onboarding, quarterly restore validation, monthly license reconciliation, successor-ready documentation).
- Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace fluency; multi-site focus (offices, labs, warehouses, field sites); co-managed model; Insurance Readiness Review as a fixed-fee entry product; AI governance.
- Existing demand assets: IT Ownership Scorecard, MSP evaluation guide, executive checklist, insights library, 5.0 Google rating.
- Scale: a small, profitable-looking firm (CEO-stated revenue ~$1.5–2M; verify before quoting) with no dedicated sales function yet.

## The opportunity to drive growth

The next $3–5M of revenue has to come from new logos of 50–300 users. At Ironguard's pricing a single logo is
$90K–$700K of ARR. That is a mid-market hunter's game: fewer, larger, relationship-driven deals with owners, COOs
and CFOs, supported by senior engineers on every call and a product that is unusually easy to defend on value.

## The freedom to influence strategy

The first commercial hire chooses the CRM, the prospecting system, the partner channels, the offer packaging and,
over time, the people. The CEO co-sells on executive calls and owns delivery. Decisions are made in a conversation,
not a committee.

## Earning substantially on results

Uncapped commission on new recurring revenue, accelerators above threshold, project commission, and a ramp draw.
Three published scenarios (economics model) range from a variable-heavy plan to a premium package for a proven
producer. Earnings are a function of what the person brings in, and the CEO is flexible for exceptional talent
within a financially sustainable plan.

## The prospect of leadership

A Builder-archetype hire who proves personal production can earn the mandate to hire and lead the sales team as
the company scales. This is a path, not a promise; titles follow results.

## Meaningful long-term participation

Long-term incentives tied to retained new ARR (cash LTIP or phantom equity) are under consideration by the CEO
and would be formalized in writing before any offer. Nothing about ownership may be implied in outreach.

## What we ask in return

Integrity with prior employers' confidential information, accountability for a pipeline the CEO can see, and the
willingness to sell personally for years, not just manage.

## Obstacles we will name ourselves (credibility)

Small base; modest brand; delivery capacity that must be grown in step with sales; a founder-led company with one
executive. Strong candidates will respect being told this first.
