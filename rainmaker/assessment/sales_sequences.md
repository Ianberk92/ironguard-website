## Sequence 4 — Post-meeting follow-up (manual; drafted from the call transcript)

Subject: What we heard, and the next step
Three parts: (1) what you told us is growing and breaking, in your words; (2) the two or three gaps we would look at first; (3) the next step with a date and who attends. Never a proposal in the first follow-up.

## Sequence 5 — Quiet-period nurture (monthly)

One insight article per month from the site (what managed IT actually costs; the cheapest laptop lifecycle; the ticket-closed problem), two sentences of context, no ask.

## Rules

- Every message names a specific fact about the recipient's company (site, renewal, trigger). No fact, no send.
- One ask per email. Short. No stock phrases.
- Always the published pricing link; never a discount.
- Replies route to the CEO the same day; the AI chief of staff drafts the response for approval.
