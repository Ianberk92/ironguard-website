Straight answers

San Diego IT support questions, answered plainly.

What does managed IT cost in San Diego?

With us: starting at $150 per user per month, listed publicly on the pricing page. Engagements are priced between $150 and $200 per user depending on security requirements, sites and onsite needs. The difference is we tell you ours before the sales call.

Do you only serve Carlsbad?

No. Carlsbad is home base. We serve all of San Diego County: Oceanside, Vista, San Marcos, Encinitas, Escondido, Sorrento Valley, and downtown San Diego.

We already have an IT person. Is that a problem?

It is a head start. Our co-managed model keeps your person in charge of the keys while we carry the load they never get time for.

What is the catch on published pricing?

There is not one, but there is a floor: 25 users minimum, onboarding is a fixed-fee project with a 50% deposit and is never waived, and we put everything in writing. The FAQ covers what is not included.
