Straight answers

What does managed IT cost in San Diego?

With us: $150 per user per month, listed publicly on the pricing page. Industry surveys put typical San Diego managed IT between $100 and $250 per user depending on scope — the difference is we tell you ours before the sales call.

Do you only serve Carlsbad?

No — Carlsbad is home base. We serve all of San Diego County: Oceanside, Vista, San Marcos, Encinitas, Escondido, Sorrento Valley, and downtown San Diego.

We already have an IT person. Is that a problem?

It's a head start. Our co-managed model keeps your person in charge of the keys while we carry the load they never get time for.

There isn't one, but there is a floor: 25 users minimum, onboarding is never waived, and we put everything in writing. The FAQ covers what's not included.
