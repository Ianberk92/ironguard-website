What usually happens

The tenant gets set up once, then drifts for years.

Licenses accumulate. Old accounts linger. SharePoint or Shared Drives grow into a jungle nobody can navigate. Teams and Chat sprawl becomes its own filing problem. Security defaults from 2021 quietly stay in place. Every one of these is small on its own. Together they become cost, risk and daily friction.

Two stacks, one standard

Microsoft 365 and Google Workspace, administered the same way.

Same identity rules, same device standard, same same-day offboarding, same monthly license reconciliation. The products differ; the ownership does not.

On Microsoft 365

  • Entra ID: identity, MFA, conditional access and role-based admin
  • Intune: device enrollment, compliance, encryption and app policy
  • Exchange Online: mail flow, anti-phishing and spoofing protection
  • Teams, SharePoint and OneDrive: structure, sharing rules and retention
  • Licensing: the right mix, unused seats reclaimed, renewals on a calendar

On Google Workspace

  • Cloud Identity: identity, 2-Step Verification, context-aware access and admin roles
  • Google endpoint management: device enrollment, compliance, encryption and app policy
  • Gmail: mail routing, SPF, DKIM and DMARC, anti-phishing and spoofing protection
  • Drive, Shared Drives, Meet and Chat: structure, sharing rules and retention
  • Licensing: the right edition mix, unused seats reclaimed, renewals on a calendar
