What usually happens

Microsoft 365 gets set up once, then drifts for years.

Licenses accumulate. Old accounts linger. SharePoint grows into a jungle nobody can navigate. Teams sprawl becomes its own filing problem. Security defaults from 2021 quietly stay in place. Every one of these is small on its own—together they become cost, risk, and daily friction.

What we own

Administration and governance, not just admin access.

Tenant administration

Day-to-day administration of Microsoft 365, Entra ID, and Intune, with changes documented and reversible.

Identity & device policy

Conditional access, device compliance, and enrollment standards that work for office, remote, and field employees.

Collaboration structure

Teams, SharePoint, and OneDrive organized around how the business works—with sharing rules people can follow.

Licensing & cost control

The right license mix, unused seats reclaimed, renewals on a calendar, and plain-language cost recommendations.

Data lifecycle

Retention, backup posture, and offboarding that preserves company knowledge when people leave.

Roadmap decisions

Clear recommendations on new Microsoft capabilities—what to adopt, what to skip, and when it is worth it.

The outcome

A clean tenant is a quiet competitive advantage.

When identity, devices, and collaboration are actually governed, everything downstream gets cheaper and faster: onboarding takes hours instead of weeks, client security reviews stop being fire drills, audits have answers on the first ask, and finance can finally see what the environment costs — and why.
