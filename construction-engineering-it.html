When this fits

The work moves. The IT has to move with it.

Project-based companies outgrow ordinary IT support in a predictable way. A new office opens and the internet is not ready. A job-site trailer gets whatever router was available that day. Project files live in three places. An owner or an insurer sends a security questionnaire and nobody is sure who answers it.

None of that is a helpdesk problem. It is an ownership problem: nobody holds the standard, the schedule and the result at the same time.

Ironguard’s founder spent a decade leading IT inside engineering and construction companies. The way we run IT comes from that seat: every site built the same way, every launch on a schedule, every area with one accountable owner.

What we take on

Offices, job sites and the people moving between them.

Managed service starts at 25 users. If you have an internal IT lead, we work through them, as owned lanes, not around them.

Sites and infrastructure

  • One network design for every location, sized by site type
  • New office and job-site launches run from a dated checklist
  • Internet circuits, failover and the carrier relationship
  • Every closet documented, labeled and photographed
  • Hardware lifecycle and a renewal calendar finance can plan around

People and security

  • Employee support for office and field staff, the same at every site
  • Onboarding and offboarding that follow crews as projects start and end
  • Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace administration
  • A security baseline: identity and MFA, devices, email and backup
  • Help preparing for cyber insurance applications and renewals
