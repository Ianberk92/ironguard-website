# Ironguard IT — Logo System: "The Held Point"

## Rationale
Two structural jaws grip a solid square: **whatever this is, it is held.** The mark
enacts "IT that takes ownership" instead of illustrating it — heavy rectilinear
strokes carry the iron/structure cue with no shield, padlock, circuit, or monogram.
Selected from 20 concepts and 5 refined finalists, scored by two independent design
critiques (brand strategy + production); refinements applied from both: square
center (rectilinear consistency, embroidery-safe), one modular unit governing all
gaps, off-axis openings with 180° rotational symmetry (interlocking jaws, not a
viewfinder), and a small-size variant with an enlarged center.

## Geometry
100-unit grid. Stroke = 12 units; every gap = 12 units (one module). Held square =
24 units (28 in the small variant, arms shortened to preserve clearance). The two
jaws are identical under 180° rotation.

## Color
| Use | Jaws | Held square |
|---|---|---|
| Primary on light | Carbon `#0E1B2A` | Forge Orange `#E4572E` |
| Reversed on dark | Cloud `#F6F7F8` | Forge Orange `#E4572E` |
| One-color | single color throughout (black/white files provided) |

Never recolor the square to green, blue, or any non-brand color. Flat color only —
no gradients, bevels, shadows, or outlines.

## Typography
Wordmark: **Sora SemiBold (600)**, tracking +0.5, "IT" in Sora Regular at
`#5c6770` (light) / Steel `#AAB4BE` (dark). Fallback stack: Avenir Next, Segoe UI,
sans-serif. SVG lockups keep live text for editability; for print vendors without
Sora, supply the PNG exports or outline the text before handoff.

## Clear space & minimum sizes
- Clear space: ≥ half the symbol width on all sides (blueprint rule preserved).
- Symbol minimum: 24 px standard; **use `symbol-small*.svg` at 16–23 px** (favicon,
  tab icons, tiny UI).
- Horizontal lockup minimum: 120 px wide. Stacked lockup minimum: 88 px wide.
- Embroidery: symbol ≥ 20 mm; the 12-unit module keeps all gaps ≥ 1.5 mm at that size.
