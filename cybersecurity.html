The real risk

The worst gap is the task everyone assumed someone else handled.

Most growing companies do not lack security tools. They lack a single owner for the whole picture. The MSP manages antivirus. A vendor sold the firewall. Someone set up MFA two years ago. Nobody can say today who has access to what, which devices are unmanaged, or what actually happens when something looks wrong.

  • Accounts stay active after people leave.
  • MFA exists, but exceptions and legacy sign-ins quietly bypass it.
  • Devices connect to company data with no management or encryption.
  • Email security settings were configured once and never revisited.
  • An alert fires and it is not clear who investigates, decides, and communicates.
What we own

A security baseline built into daily operations.

Identity & access

One identity standard: MFA enforced, conditional access, least-privilege roles, and access reviews that actually happen.

Endpoint protection

Every laptop and mobile device enrolled, encrypted, patched, monitored, and recoverable—including field and remote devices.

Email & collaboration

Phishing and spoofing protection, safe external sharing, and sensible defaults your people do not have to think about.

Data & access control

Clear rules for where company data lives, who can reach it, and what leaves the building—without breaking how people work.

Incident response

When something looks wrong, there is an owner, a process, and plain-language communication to leadership—not silence.

Compliance support

Practical help with cyber-insurance questionnaires, client security reviews, and the controls auditors ask about.
