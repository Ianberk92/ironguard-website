When this fits

The science moves fast. The IT has to keep up and keep records.

Life sciences companies live with a strange mix. Lab instruments run on PCs that cannot be patched like ordinary laptops. Years of research data sit on a share nobody has tried to restore. A funding round lands and you need to hire fast. Then an investor or partner sends a security questionnaire, and the quality team asks who signed off the last change to a validated system.

Each of those is manageable on its own. The trouble starts when the lab, the office and the manufacturing floor each have a different answer, and nobody owns all of them together.

We take that over and run it as one system. Ironguard IT is based in Carlsbad, in Southern California, a short drive from the labs of Sorrento Valley and Torrey Pines, and supports companies across California.

What we take on

Lab, office and floor on one standard, each in its own lane.

Managed service starts at 25 users. If you have an internal IT lead or a lab systems specialist, we work through them, as owned lanes, not around them.

Labs, data and quality

  • Lab instruments and their PCs on their own network, kept apart from corporate
  • Research data backed up on a schedule, with restores tested and written down
  • Change control records for IT changes to validated and quality systems
  • 21 CFR Part 11 validation support: access control, audit trails, account reviews and IT documentation
  • One network standard across the lab, the office and manufacturing

People and security

  • Onboarding that keeps pace when a funding round turns into a hiring plan
  • Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace administration and licensing
  • A security baseline: identity and MFA, devices, email and backup
  • Answers and evidence for investor, partner and customer security reviews
  • Instrument and software vendors managed, so your scientists do not have to chase them
Your first 90 days with us

What happens after you hire us.

A typical start. Your own plan is written down, with names and dates, before any work begins.

  1. Week 1

    We learn the lab before we touch it.

    • Walk every lab, office and manufacturing area with you
    • List every instrument PC, what it runs and who owns it
    • Find where research data actually lives, and check the last backup
    • Take over admin accounts and put them in your company’s name

    Where you are: A written map of your environment, and the three biggest risks named.

  2. Days 1–30

    Fix what could hurt you this quarter.

    • MFA on every account, starting with admins and finance
    • Research data backed up, and a real restore tested
    • Instrument PCs moved onto their own network, with only the connections they need
    • A change log started, so every IT change to a quality system has a record

    Where you are: Your data is recoverable, and nothing changes in the lab without a record.

  3. Days 31–60

    Set the standard everyone follows.

    • A new-hire checklist: laptop, accounts and access ready on day one
    • Devices enrolled and managed, with encryption on every laptop
    • Access reviews for validated and quality systems, written up for your QA team
    • A security pack ready for investor and partner questionnaires

    Where you are: Hiring after a raise stops being an IT emergency.

  4. Days 61–90

    Plan ahead of the science.

    • A 12-month roadmap: new lab space, the next site, equipment refresh
    • Disaster recovery plan written and walked through with your team
    • Vendor contracts and renewals on one calendar
    • Your first quarterly review: what changed, what is next, what it costs

    Where you are: IT that is documented, owned and ready for the next round of diligence.

Common questions

Straight answers for life sciences and medtech companies.

Can you support lab instrument PCs that cannot be updated?

Yes. Many instrument PCs run older software that the instrument vendor has to approve before anything changes. We put them on their own network, limit what they can reach, back up their data, and work with the instrument vendor on any update.

Can you help with 21 CFR Part 11 validation?

Yes. We run the IT side of your validation work end to end: access control, audit trail and backup settings, account reviews, change control records for every IT change to a quality system, and the documentation and evidence your validation packages need. Your quality team approves and signs off; we coordinate with them and with the software vendor.

We just closed a funding round. Can you keep up with hiring?

Yes. Every new hire gets the same checklist: laptop, accounts and access ready before day one. When people leave, their access is removed the same day.

Can you help with investor and partner security questionnaires?

Yes. We build a security pack with your policies and the evidence behind them, then help you answer each questionnaire honestly. Policy and audit support is included in the Managed + Security level; on other levels it is scoped in writing.

What does it cost?

Three published levels, $115 to $200 per user per month. Companies with quality systems, partner security reviews or compliance requirements usually fit the top level, Managed + Security. Hardware, lab moves and major projects are scoped separately, in writing, before work begins.
