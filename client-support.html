Help us help you

What to include.

You will always know who owns your issue, what is happening, and what comes next.

  • What you were trying to do, and what happened instead.
  • Who and how many people are affected.
  • When it started, and whether anything changed recently.
  • A screenshot of any error message, if you have one.
