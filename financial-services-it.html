When this fits

Your clients trust you with their money. Everyone wants proof.

Financial firms answer to a lot of people. The SEC and FINRA expect written policies you actually follow. The amended Regulation S-P expects an incident response plan and real oversight of your vendors. Custodians and institutional clients send due-diligence questionnaires. Advisers send statements by email because the portal is clumsy. And when you buy another practice, its IT arrives with it.

Your chief compliance officer should not also be your IT department.

We take the technology side over and run it as one system, alongside your compliance team, not instead of it. Ironguard IT is based in Carlsbad, in Southern California, and supports companies across California.

What we take on

Controls you can show. Systems your advisers like using.

Managed service starts at 25 users. We work with your chief compliance officer and any outside compliance consultant, as owned lanes, not around them.

Security and oversight

  • MFA on every account, every device and every remote connection
  • Your Regulation S-P program run end to end: incident response plan, testing, vendor oversight and evidence
  • Vendor oversight: a list of who holds client data, and the evidence they protect it
  • Email and messaging archiving set up for your books-and-records rules
  • Answers and evidence for custodian, client and insurer due-diligence questionnaires

Advisers and offices

  • Secure client portals and file sharing that replace emailed statements

  • Backup and disaster recovery for client records, with restores tested
Your first 90 days with us

What happens after you hire us.

A typical start. Your own plan is written down, with names and dates, before any work begins.

  1. Week 1

    We learn how you work and what you have promised.

    • Meet your compliance officer and read your written policies
    • List every system and vendor that holds client data
    • Check MFA, email archiving and backups as they are today
    • Take over admin accounts and put them in your firm’s name

    Where you are: A written map of your environment, matched to your own policies.

  2. Days 1–30

    Close the gaps an examiner would find first.

    • MFA on every account, with no exceptions for senior advisers
    • Email and messaging archiving checked against your retention rules
    • Client records backed up, and a real restore tested
    • Your incident response plan drafted, with names and phone numbers

    Where you are: The basics an examiner or client checks first are in place.

  3. Days 31–60

    Make the secure way the easy way.

    • A secure client portal or file-sharing setup your advisers will use
    • Vendor oversight file started: contracts, security answers, review dates
    • Devices enrolled, encrypted and patched, including home offices
    • A new-hire and leaver checklist, so access always matches the job

    Where you are: Advisers stop emailing statements because the secure way is faster.

  4. Days 61–90

    Be ready to be asked.

    • A tabletop run of your incident response plan with your team
    • A security pack for custodian, client and insurer questionnaires
    • A plan for bringing the next acquired office onto your standard
    • Your first quarterly review: what changed, what is next, what it costs

    Where you are: When a client or custodian sends a questionnaire, you answer with evidence.

Common questions

Straight answers for financial services firms.

Can you run the technology side of our SEC and FINRA compliance?

Yes, end to end. We assess where you stand, put the controls in place and manage them (MFA, email archiving, access reviews, incident response, vendor oversight), write the procedures, collect the evidence, monitor continuously and support you through exams and audits. We work alongside your chief compliance officer, who owns your compliance program.

How do you help with the amended Regulation S-P?

We run it end to end: a written incident response plan to find, contain and respond to unauthorized access to customer information, tested with your team; the vendor oversight file showing which service providers hold customer data and how they protect it; and the evidence your examiner will ask for. Your compliance officer and counsel make the final calls on policy and customer notices.

Can you set up email and messaging archiving?

Yes. We set up and check archiving for email and approved messaging tools, matched to the retention rules your compliance officer sets, and make sure records can be found when someone asks for them.

We are buying another practice. Can you bring its IT over?

Yes. We review the acquired office’s accounts, devices and data first, then move people onto your standard in planned steps, so client service keeps running during the move. Integrations are scoped separately, in writing.

What does it cost?

Three published levels, $115 to $200 per user per month. Firms with regulatory, client or insurer security requirements usually fit the top level, Managed + Security. Hardware, office integrations and major projects are scoped separately, in writing, before work begins.
