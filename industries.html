The common thread

The industry matters less than the operating shape.

Every best-fit client looks the same in the ways that count. If three of these five describe your company, we already speak your language, whatever the industry.

  • 50–500 people. Large enough that IT is a function, not a favor.
  • More than one location. Offices, labs, warehouses, job sites or a distributed workforce.
  • A Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace environment. Identity, email, devices and files on one platform.
  • Customers or insurers with real security expectations. Questionnaires, reviews and renewals that need evidence.
  • Growth that broke the informal way of doing things. Onboarding, licensing and vendors can no longer be handled ad hoc.
Getting started

Three steps from first call to fully managed.

Step one

A 30-minute conversation

What is growing, what is breaking, and what you need someone to own. No slide deck.

Step two

Environment review

We look at your Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace tenant, devices, network and security posture, then write up what we found and what it will take.

Step three

Transition plan and go-live

Onboarding is a fixed-fee project (50% deposit), then a fixed monthly rate with clear standards and a named owner from day one. Managed plans start at 25 users.

Common questions

Straight answers for multi-site operators.

Do you support field sites, warehouses and labs, not just offices?

Yes. Office, warehouse, lab and field sites: firewalls, Wi-Fi, switching, internet circuits and the projects that tie them together, on one standard.

Are you Microsoft-only?

No. Identity, email, devices, file storage and licensing on Microsoft 365 or Google Workspace, configured properly and governed so it stays that way.

We have internal IT. Can you work alongside them?

Yes. Co-managed IT is for companies with an internal team that needs backup: we carry the security, escalation and project work they never get time for.

Is there a minimum size?

Managed plans start at 25 users. Pricing starts at $115 per user per month and is scoped in writing before any work begins.
